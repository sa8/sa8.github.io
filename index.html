I am a researcher with 10 years of experience in applied cryptography and decentralised systems. I have a PhD from the Information Security Group at University College London, where my thesis on decentralisation and trust in cryptocurrencies was supervised by Sarah Meiklejohn and George Danezis. Before UCL, I studied Engineering at CentraleSupelec in Paris.

I have worked as a research scientist at Protocol Labs (on the security of the Filecoin consensus protocol) and at Scroll, and as a research intern at Meta on their Diem project. I have also founded a startup in the decentralised AI space.

I am currently interested in AI safety and security.
